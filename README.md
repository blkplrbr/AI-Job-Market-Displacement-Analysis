# AI-Job-Market-Displacement-Analysis
# Project Overview
A comprehensive data analysis of the 2026 AI-driven job cut tracker. The goal of this project is to visualize the macroeconomic impact of artificial intelligence on the workforce, identifying which industries, job roles, and regions are most vulnerable to automation.

## The Business Problem
As AI capabilities accelerate, policymakers and workforce development agencies need to understand exactly where the displacement is happening. Are jobs being lost to AI in tech? Or is it hitting customer service, media, and finance harder? 

## The Analysis (Power BI / Python)
1.  **The Industry Heatmap:** Visualized which sectors are experiencing the highest volume of AI-related layoffs.
2.  **The Automation Reason:** Categorized the layoffs by the specific AI technology replacing them.
3.  **The Retraining Opportunity:** Cross-referenced the lost jobs with the fastest-growing AI skills to identify what training programs are needed to help displaced workers re-enter the workforce.

## Business Impact
*   Provides a clear, data-driven picture of the AI labor market shift.
*   Helps guide government policy and corporate HR strategies for upskilling and reskilling.
